## Complete bibliography

### [BD00] Bhatia and Davis

R. Bhatia and C. Davis, *A better bound on the variance*, American Mathematical Monthly **107** (2000), no.4, 353–357. DOI: [10.1080/00029890.2000.12005203](https://doi.org/10.1080/00029890.2000.12005203). Original record: [JSTOR 2589180](https://www.jstor.org/stable/2589180).

The source of the classical interval/mean variance estimate. Metadata and the opening statement are available; complete publisher access was restricted. The short proof in the manuscript removes any dependence on inaccessible proof details. The reference is not presented as an assertion of historical first discovery.

### [Bor07] Borcea

J. Borcea, *Equilibrium points of logarithmic potentials induced by positive charge distributions. I. Generalized de Bruijn–Springer relations*, Transactions of the American Mathematical Society **359** (2007), no.7, 3209–3237. DOI: [10.1090/S0002-9947-07-04251-1](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9947-07-04251-1). [Author version](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0601519).

The discussion refers to Section 3.2, Theorems 4 and 5, and explicitly distinguishes its equilibrium-point-to-charge distance from the reverse direction needed here. The DOI ends in **04251-1**. An unrelated nearby AMS DOI is not a substitute.

### [BX99] Brown and Xiang

J. E. Brown and G. Xiang, *Proof of the Sendov conjecture for polynomials of degree at most eight*, Journal of Mathematical Analysis and Applications **232** (1999), no.2, 272–292. DOI: [10.1006/jmaa.1999.6267](https://doi.org/10.1006/jmaa.1999.6267).

Used for the documented historical degree-eight result, not as a proof of the stronger variance assertion. Title, journal, volume, pages and DOI were cross-checked with primary bibliographic material.

### [Deg14] Dégot

J. Dégot, *Sendov conjecture for high degree polynomials*, Proceedings of the American Mathematical Society **142** (2014), no.4, 1337–1349. DOI: [10.1090/S0002-9939-2014-11888-0](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9939-2014-11888-0). [Author version](https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.4126).

The introduction qualifies the dependence on the distinguished zero; it does not conflate this with Tao's later uniform sufficiently-large-degree theorem.

### [Hay67] Hayman

W. K. Hayman, *Research problems in function theory*, The Athlone Press, University of London, London, 1967.

Used as the historical problem collection, consistently with the original historical account in KPPSS. No unverified problem number or page has been added. No DOI is invented for this edition.

### [KPPSS11] Khavinson, Pereira, Putinar, Saff, and Shimorin

D. Khavinson, R. Pereira, M. Putinar, E. B. Saff, and S. Unpublished Borcea formulations are attributed to this recorded account, not assigned invented publication details.

The manuscript explicitly explains that its disk-based polarization estimate and certificate cannot be imported under a mere second-moment hypothesis. The source is not described as journal-published.

## Sorting, formatting, and citation checks

The order is by author surname, not by citation key. In particular Tang precedes Tao. Journal names use consistent AMS-style abbreviations; volume is bold; issue, year, page range and available DOI are consistently recorded. Books, chapters, preprints and the blog entry are identified by their actual publication type. All 16 entries are cited in the text; no undefined citation keys remain.

The search for simplifications was performed at the level of each lemma, as recorded in `AUDIT_REPORT.md`. It was not an exhaustive proof of novelty. Failure to locate a prior version of a specific auxiliary inequality is not represented as evidence that no such version exists.
